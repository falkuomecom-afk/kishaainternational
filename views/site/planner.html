What “live” and “verified” mean here

Live flight fares apply only when an approved airline API returns a quote for the exact itinerary, cabin and baggage with a booking timestamp. Official statutory rules carry a government source, effective date and reviewer. Indicative living costs are maintained statistical averages with observation dates, refreshed on a semi-annual cycle.

Rules we deliberately withhold

Where a statutory amount is pending reconfirmation, the planner shows “more information needed” and refers the case to a counselor instead of computing from a stale figure. That is why a few destinations show an empty funds card.
