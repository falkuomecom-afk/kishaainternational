How we keep this honest

  • Aggregate scores and counts come from platform APIs — never entered by hand
  • Each card shows the platform name, reviewer display name and a deep link
  • Reviews removed or edited at source are synchronised and disappear on the next refresh
  • Staff can hide an item for relevance or tone, with the reason and moderator recorded in the audit trail
  • Where a provider is not connected, the page serves the last cached snapshot and says so
